Programmable photonics

Programmable photonics covers any photonic integrated circuit whose behaviour can be changed after fabrication, by electrically tuning phase shifters, couplers or resonators. The same chip can then be reconfigured, re-calibrated or corrected for fabrication errors.

Programmable circuits range from single tunable devices, such as switches, tunable filters and delay lines, to application-specific circuits, such as matrix multipliers, beamformers and switching fabrics. At the most flexible end are general-purpose waveguide meshes, which can be programmed to perform many different functions.

This site collects interactive simulations of these devices and circuits. Each one has a 3D view, live plots and the model behind it.
